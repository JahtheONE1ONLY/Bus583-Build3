What I would not trust

The original 31.4% Office Supplies margin is too uncertain for an investment decision. The 65 profit-greater-than-sales rows contribute about 25% of total reported profit. Excluding them lowers the reported Office Supplies margin to about 23.9%, but that exclusion also needs justification.
